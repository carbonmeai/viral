# Approach: narrated ranking

**Descriptor:** hook `claim` · voices `interleaved` · shape `countdown` · language `plain` · ending `real-payoff` ·
format `narrated`.

Taken from the creator's approved "Top 5 Animal Comebacks" (2:12, five videos from The Dodo and We Love Animals). It
replaced the list-style top 10 and top 6 (each clip with its own sound only), which "could not properly tell a fully
engaging story": every entry was 15 s of out-of-context talk. A narrated ranking is a narrated short across several
videos, one entry per video.

**When it fits:** "top N / best X" ideas where each video is one complete story (a rescue, a comeback, a
transformation, a record). The list style (`ranking`) stays for clips that explain themselves visually.

## Shape

```
NARRATOR  the hook: "These five animals were given almost no chance. Number one had already given up."
NARRATOR  "Number five, [who + how bad]… [the care]…"   → REAL  1–2 lines that answer the narrator
NARRATOR  "Number four, …"                               → REAL  …
…
NARRATOR  "Number one, [the worst before]… [the care]…"
REAL      #1's reveal: the real payoff take over the after footage (about 20 s, one or two takes)
```

## Script rules

- **Five entries by default**, counted down 5 → 1, each **about 20–25 s**, so each is a full mini-story: **shocking
  before → being cared for → the reveal**. The care step is required.
- **Every entry opens on "Number N,"**: the rank card ("#5" in red, "#1" in yellow) appears on that word.
- **The hook teases #1** ("Number one had already given up.") and opens the loop the last entry closes.
- **Real numbers in every entry** ("ten years on hooves so long they curled", "two hours to shave her", "for seven
  days they lifted him with a tractor"), all from that entry's video.
- **1–2 real lines per entry**, cut in where they answer the narrator ("She looked like a cotton ball with legs",
  "That's why I named her Halo, because we weren't sure if she was going to pull through"). Each entry's real lines
  come from that entry's own video, on whole sentences.
- **#1's reveal is the ending**: its real payoff take over the after footage, not narration.
- After it comes the closing question: "Which comeback got you? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe for more."
- Rank by how dramatic the turnaround is; mix the subjects (species, places).

## Shots

- Each entry's shots come from its own video, in its order: before → care → after.
- Our captions sit low, in the blur under the picture, away from the videos' own burned-in subtitles.
- Look at the first and last frame of every shot: compilations cut to other subjects, and recaps replay the before
  inside the after.

## Finding its videos (required)

Each entry is **one 1–6 minute video about one subject** with **over a million views** (The Dodo, We Love Animals,
Hope For Paws), **real interview lines in its transcript** (someone telling the story, not just music), and **clear
before and after footage**. Reject long compilations, music-only vlogs and re-uploads: a 20-minute "ER dog" video was
mostly music and merch plugs.

## Post text

- Title: a tease of #1, under 40 characters, may end with one emoji: "He Had Already Given Up… 😢". Alternates: "5 Animals
  Given No Chance", "Ranking Animal Comebacks: Top 5".
- Description: two sentences with each entry's most extreme real detail, no spoiled ending, a last line that teases
  #1: "Number one will get you."
- Footage credits per entry (Viral adds them).
