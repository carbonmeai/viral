# Approach: cut-down

**Descriptor:** hook `quote` · voices `creator-only` · shape `single-arc` · language `plain` · ending
`hard-payoff` · format `cut-down`.

**When it fits:** podcasts, interviews, debates, talk shows, rants, tutorial tips, sports calls, gaming commentary
and stand-up: anywhere the speakers' own words are the content and the visuals matter less. For build or experiment
videos with slow talking but great visuals, prefer a narrated approach; if the talking turns out weak but the
visuals are strong, switch to narrated.

## Plan: sections

A cut-down short is a list of **sections**, each one stretch of the source with its story beat (see
`story-arc`):

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `beat` | `hook`, `rising_action`, `conflict`, `comeback`, `payoff` (or `climax`, `resolution`) |
| `start`, `end` | **source** seconds; snapped outward to word boundaries, so transcript times are fine |

- **Sections play in the order given, not source order.** The hook usually comes from late in the video.
- **Use several sections with the same beat** to jump-cut filler out of it. Every section boundary is a jump
  cut, which helps the pacing.
- A section may set its own framing or crop when the speaker moves or a screen share needs the full frame.

## Rules

- **Find 3–6 candidates first**: self-contained moments with a clear open loop (a bold claim, a story with a twist,
  a heated disagreement, a surprising number, a reveal). Score each on the radar and build the best one.
- **Hook first: the creator's single strongest line**, wherever it is in the video, as the first section.
  Start on its first word, not on a breath or "so".
- **Whole sentences only.** Every section starts on the first word of a sentence and ends on the last word of one
  (only a pause over 1.5 s ends a sentence). Never end on a half-answer: if a reply runs long, keep it whole and cut
  somewhere else.
- **Self-contained:** drop anything that needs the long video ("like I said", "in part two").
- **The sections end on the payoff's last word.** No "so yeah", no trailing filler. After it comes the closing
  question card (a comment question plus subscribe, see `story-arc`), never a section of the speaker asking.
- **30–120 seconds**, long enough for the exchange to land whole.
- **Hook text:** the open loop in 7 words or fewer, readable with the sound off, shown over the hook.
- **Never use excluded ranges** (sponsor reads, ads, end screens).
- Score the result on the virality radar (`story-arc`) and say which axis is weakest.

## Shorts from several videos

- **Every section names its video** (`video`: its V number, as the brief labels them) and uses that video's own
  seconds. Check the storyboard's V labels: a wrong video with a valid time plays the wrong footage.
- **A cut between videos is allowed anywhere,** but the first section from each new video needs a **context
  cut**: a line that says who or where this is ("Meanwhile at her party…", the person naming the thing), so the
  jump makes sense without a narrator.
- **Use the fewest videos the story needs,** and let one video carry the hook rather than splitting it.
- **Each video's excluded ranges are its own.** Keep every section clear of the ranges listed for its video.
